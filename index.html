Division podiums
One winner per division, so a Div 4 player is only ever measured against other Div 4 players. Your division is fixed at the rating you started the month on — climbing during the month never moves you into a harder division mid-prize. Same rules otherwise: best 3 sessions decides it, total is the tiebreak, three sessions to qualify, and a division needs at least three qualified players before it pays anything — winning a field of two is not a result. Players still Settling when the month opened have no reliable division and are listed apart.
